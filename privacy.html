Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

media-engine-uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner on the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos, captions and comment replies to the owner's own channels.
  • Read-only access to those channels' own videos and YouTube Analytics statistics.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how those videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored privately and encrypted, and are never published.

Google API Services

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
